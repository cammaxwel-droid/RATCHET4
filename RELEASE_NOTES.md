Complete experimental Ratchet-4 V1 review package: runnable Windows/Linux demos, C++17 source, automated tests, design notes, original evidence, and the NS3 warning investigation.

Download **P-RNG-Review.zip**, extract it, and open **START_DEMO.cmd**. Use **COPY_TO_DOCUMENTS.cmd** to copy the review package into a new Documents folder.

V1 remains experimental. The warning investigation found evidence of small-sample calibration problems in NS3, including a failure on an external control stream. This does not establish V1's security, fairness, or production suitability. The engine is unchanged. Real-money deployment is not approved.

Windows executables were previously executed under Wine, not native Windows 11. The Documents helper has not been executed on the user's PC. Source and project documents use 0BSD; third-party notices are included.
